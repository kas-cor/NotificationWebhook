A SIMPLE FLOW

From notification to your workflow.

Use your own endpoint to connect Android notifications to tools and workflows you already run.

01

Connect your endpoint

Enter a webhook URL and, if needed, an optional Bearer token. Send a test POST to check the connection.

02

Choose what to forward

Forward notifications from selected apps or all apps. Add case-insensitive rules for titles, text, app names, or package names.

03

Inspect each delivery

See recent attempts, HTTP status, and time in the local history. Turn an entry into an exclusion rule in one tap.

MADE TO FIT YOUR SETUP

Useful control, without another inbox.

A focused Android utility: configure the destination, shape the payload flow, and check what happened.

Structured JSON

App name and package, title, text, category, channel, priority, notification ID, and timestamps.

App and content filters

Allow specific apps, skip ongoing notifications, or exclude matching content with rules you control.

Local send history

The app keeps the latest 50 webhook attempts with result, HTTP code, and timestamp on your device.

Optional Bearer auth

Add a token to outgoing requests when your webhook endpoint requires authorization.

Background support

A foreground keep-alive service and restart handling help maintain the listener. Some Android manufacturers may require extra battery settings.

Optional promo workflow

With a compatible classification server and agent flow, an explicit dismiss verdict can clear a promo notification. On errors or keep verdicts, it stays visible.

YOUR PHONE · YOUR ENDPOINT

Keep the plumbing in your hands.

NotifWebhook sends data to the URL you configure; it does not provide a hosted webhook service. Review the permissions and source code before installing, and only forward notifications you are comfortable processing.
